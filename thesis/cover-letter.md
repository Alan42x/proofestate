# Cover Letter for Journal of Information Security and Applications

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit the manuscript entitled **"Privacy-Preserving Compliance Enforcement for Blockchain-Based Real-Estate Lending Using Zero-Knowledge Proofs"** for consideration as a Research Article in the *Journal of Information Security and Applications*.

This manuscript presents ProofEstate, a privacy-preserving compliance architecture and reproducible prototype for blockchain-based real-estate lending. The work addresses a practical information-security problem: public blockchains can make lending workflows auditable, but direct publication of property valuations and requested loan principals would expose sensitive financial data. ProofEstate resolves this tension for a bounded loan-to-value policy by combining MiMC commitments, a Groth16 zero-knowledge proof, explicit on-chain public-signal binding, role-gated smart contracts, and a trusted oracle service.

The submission fits JISA because it focuses on a security application rather than a purely theoretical cryptographic contribution. The manuscript contributes an end-to-end system design, a clear threat model, implemented mitigations against proof substitution and stale compliance state, and reproducible empirical evidence. The evaluation includes 28 passing contract tests, 12 passing oracle API tests, 6 passing frontend tests, a successful local end-to-end lending workflow, circuit-size inspection, gas measurement, a 30-run compliant-path benchmark, a four-case 30-run correctness matrix, bounded 10/30/50 repeated-run stability evidence, median Groth16 proving latency of 399.53 ms, and average on-chain proof-verification gas of 310,411.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been published previously. All authors have approved the submission. The software artifact and benchmark materials are available at https://github.com/Alan42x/proofestate.

Thank you for considering this manuscript. We believe it will be of interest to JISA readers working on privacy-preserving systems, blockchain security, zero-knowledge applications, financial-technology security, and secure oracle-mediated workflows.
